# Content and Trust Audit

## Content quality
- The homepage contains clear, persuasive service language and a conversion-oriented CTA flow.
- The copy is generally understandable and not overloaded with jargon.
- The most important weakness is not grammar or readability; it is missing proof and supporting evidence.
- Some claims are strong marketing assertions without external or measurable backing: “FAST”, “SECURE”, “RELIABLE”, “SEARCH READY”, “BETTER ENQUIRIES”, “MOST POPULAR”.

## Trust signals currently present
- Brand identity and logo presence
- General business proposition and service list
- About section linking to NMAS INNOVATIONS Pty Ltd
- Contact details in the footer and contact call-to-action
- Pricing structure is visible
- Hosting/support promise is visible
- Work section includes some real client brands and public business URLs

## Missing trust signals
- Case studies with outcomes and evidence
- Published testimonials with names, roles, and evidence of actual results
- Clear support and renewal terms after the included 12 months
- Proof of hosting performance, uptime, or support response times
- Client logos with proof-backed context, not just text links
- A detailed process or delivery timeline that matches the promise of a managed service
- Clear legal/privacy/terms information beyond the site itself

## Business claim classification
- VERIFIED: website is built in Next.js; basic service list is clearly visible; contact form exists; pricing packs are presented.
- BUSINESS CLAIM: “Beautiful websites, reliable hosting and ongoing support — without the technical headache.”
- NEEDS EVIDENCE: “MOST POPULAR”; “SEARCH READY”; “SECURE”; “RELIABLE”; “BETTER ENQUIRIES”; “A real person for updates, questions and practical help after launch.”
- REQUIRES CLIENT INPUT: actual client outcomes, testimonials, package popularity, specific support levels, hosting SLA, renewal costs.

## Trust status
- Trust: AMBER
- Reason: the site establishes a professional identity and offers a coherent proposition, but it is missing the proof layer that is often required to close an enquiry from a first-time visitor.

## Recommended improvements
- P1: add a “proof” section with measurable outcomes and client-specific results only where approved.
- P1: clearly separate concept work from verified client projects.
- P1: add support terms and hosting renewal details where the website suggests long-term care.
- P2: add visible business credibility cues such as disclosures, legal identity references, and privacy/terms links if they exist official externally.

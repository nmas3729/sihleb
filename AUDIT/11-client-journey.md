# Client Journey Map

## Stage 1 — Discovery
- Existing mechanism: homepage hero, service pages, SEO page titles, sitemap, and social metadata.
- Evidence: the homepage clearly states the services and positioning.
- Friction: strong brand message, but not enough proof or outcomes to help a researcher decide if SihleB is credible beyond the sales copy.
- Missing information: exact client types, sectors, and performance proof.

## Stage 2 — Trust
- Existing mechanism: brand story, about section, portfolio links, hosting/support narrative, and visible business identity.
- Evidence: the site says the company is a division of NMAS INNOVATIONS Pty Ltd and lists public-facing client businesses.
- Friction: the work section mixes real and concept projects; trust is diluted when it is unclear which are real and which are speculative.
- Missing information: testimonials, measurable outcomes, direct proof of client satisfaction.

## Stage 3 — Evaluation
- Existing mechanism: pricing pages and package cards.
- Evidence: package structure is visible and there are CTAs to enquire.
- Friction: package differences are not as transparent as they could be; long-term cost, support, and renewal information are not explicit.
- Missing information: what is included after the 12-month period, what changes with higher tiers, and what support is included.

## Stage 4 — Decision
- Existing mechanism: FAQ, process section, and final CTA.
- Evidence: the content explains general process and supports the purchase decision.
- Friction: decision anxiety remains because proof and pricing certainty are incomplete.
- Missing information: actual business-case examples, FAQs on time-to-launch, support response times, and pricing clarity.

## Stage 5 — Enquiry
- Existing mechanism: contact form and WhatsApp CTA.
- Evidence: both are clear and directly accessible.
- Friction: the form is solid but the success state and next-step message could be stronger and more explicit.
- Missing information: exactly what the user should expect after they submit the form.

## Stage 6 — Post-enquiry
- Existing mechanism: the form sends an email and also sends a confirmation email to the user.
- Evidence: the API route sends both the enquiry email and a confirmation email.
- Friction: there is no visible confirmation page or strong expectation-setting on the website itself.
- Missing information: a defined follow-up SLA or step-by-step handoff process.

## Recommended journey improvement
- Add a stronger proof layer near the hero and work sections.
- Separate actual client work from concept work.
- Improve pricing transparency and renewal information.
- Clarify the next-step experience after form submission.

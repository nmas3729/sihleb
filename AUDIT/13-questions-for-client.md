# Questions for SihleB

## Pricing and commercial clarity
1. What are the exact renewal prices for hosting, email, and website care after the included 12-month period?
2. What is included in the hosting package beyond SSL, backups, and support?
3. What exactly is included in the support plan, and what is considered an additional charge?
4. Are the packages intended to be starting prices only, or can the business provide a fixed range for each level?
5. Does “MOST POPULAR” reflect actual business demand, or is it primarily a sales positioning choice?
6. What happens if a client leaves SihleB hosting or support after the first 12 months?

## Proof and trust
7. Which client projects are approved for public case studies and which are not?
8. Do you have approved testimonials with names, roles, and project context that can be published?
9. Do you have measurable results for projects, such as conversion uplift, lead volume, or engagement improvements?
10. What actual business outcomes should the public site be allowed to claim, and which should remain unnamed?

## Product and service scope
11. Are the service pages intentionally distinct by audience, or are they overlapping and in need of consolidation?
12. Is the site intended to target local South African businesses only, or is the strategy broader than that?
13. Do you want the portfolio to show real projects only, or is concept work part of the public narrative?
14. Are there any non-public service categories, technical offer types, or enterprise packages that should be accounted for?

## Analytics and measurement
15. What business outcome should define a successful enquiry: form submission, WhatsApp click, or direct email response?
16. Which source/medium channels matter most to the business in the next 90 days?
17. Do you want a standard lead funnel for reporting: landing page → CTA click → form start → form completion → qualified lead?
18. Should analytics tracking be expanded to include link clicks, phone clicks, and package selection events?

## Process and operations
19. What is the expected follow-up SLA after a user submits the form?
20. What should the success state say to users after they submit an enquiry?
21. What is the policy for support response times and issue escalation after launch?
22. Is there a formal legal/privacy policy or terms page associated with the site that should be linked from the public pages?

## Strategy and content
23. What is the top priority for improvement: stronger proof, improved conversion, better SEO, better performance, or improved package clarity?
24. Which page should be the primary conversion page for new visitors?
25. Are there any sensitive client names or project details that must remain private even if the public work section is improved?
